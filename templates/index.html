Amla Embalic officinalis

Amla is generally known as an Indian Gooseberry. The berries of the trees are potently used for medicinal formulations due to their healing properties. Amla tree contains small berries that are round and yellowish-green in colour. Due to its several health benefits, it should be referred to as a superfood. In ancient Ayurveda, the Amla is known by a different name such as sour, nurse, immortality, and mother.
Uses: Heartburn, Anti-aging, Cancer, Heart health, Blood sugar levels, Liver damage, Immune health.
Locations: Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu.





Ark saphed Calotropis gigantia

Arka (Calotropis gigantea) is a perennial shrub growing 2.5 meter high with various branches and sub branches. Leaves are simple, opposite, sub sessile, ovate and cordate at base. It has bunch of waxy flowers that are white or lavender in color. Flowers consists of pointed five petals and a small beautiful crown, which holds the stamen. It has milky stem and contains cardiac glycosides, fatty acid and calcium oxalate.
Uses: Acne and pimples, Tooth Moving, Itching, Scorpion bite, Ulcer and pimples, Snake poison, small white marks, Disorders caused by gas.
Location: Rajasthan, Gujarat, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra.





Ashoka Saraca indica

The Ashoka plant (Saraca asoca), also known as the sacred tree, is a revered evergreen tree native to the Indian subcontinent. It is celebrated for its medicinal properties in Ayurveda and is often associated with religious and cultural significance in India. The tree produces vibrant orange-red flowers and thrives in humid and tropical regions. Known for its dense foliage, it is commonly planted in gardens and temples for its aesthetic and spiritual appeal.
Uses: Women's Health, Digestive Health, Skin and Wound Care, Anti-inflammatory Properties, Anti-depressant, Cosmetics, Natural Dye.
Location: Karnataka, Kerala, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Uttar Pradesh, Jharkhand, Tamil Nadu, Bihar.
